Working paper · October 2026

Extending Bulk-Billing Incentives to All Patients: Evidence from Australia’s November 2025 Medicare Reform

In November 2025 Medicare started paying GPs the bulk-billing incentive for every patient, not just children and concession card holders. Comparing working-age adults with children across 393 local government areas, I find the reform raised bulk billing by 6.3 percentage points, at an incentive cost of about $187 for each additional bulk-billed visit.

Master’s thesis · in progress

Pricing Battery Wear Without Usage Data

EV lessors set lease prices before they know how hard a battery will be driven. I study what that missing information costs, and what telematics data on battery health would be worth to them.
